**About me**

Hi, I'm Mauricio. I study **Software Engineering** at **UTFPR**, Cornélio Procópio campus, and I'm in my sixth semester.

I work as a **full stack** developer, mostly with **TypeScript**, and I enjoy following a project all the way from the database to the screen.

I'm currently part of **Unect Jr.** and **G4tech**, and I also contribute to the **TEDI** project, helping with the web app, the API and the documentation.

**Experience**

At **Unect Jr.**, as a backend advisor, I helped build systems for medical clinics, working on the API, business rules and database. I like to get ahead of problems and I'm always ready to help the team.

At **G4tech**, I started as an intern and was promoted to junior full stack developer. I've worked on web and mobile projects, and also on the backend, from building interfaces to integrating APIs.
